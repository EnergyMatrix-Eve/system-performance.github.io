Performance Review · FY2025–26

Eve Liu

Data Analyst & Engineer · Billing, Automation & Data Modelling

This year I focused on replacing manual, one-by-one processes with reliable automation — across ATCO B2B transactions, retail billing, market data flows and reporting — so the business can bill faster, reconcile with confidence, and see its data clearly.

Milestones

  • 1.1

    XML transformation & data ingestion pipeline

    Built automation that transforms inbound XML and ingests acknowledgement and transaction data into the EMG database, with automatic notifications routed to the relevant teams — for information, or where action is required.

    Replaced manual file handling with a single, reliable source of truth and proactive alerting.
  • 1.2

    Automated network bill calculation & reconciliation

    Automated the calculation of network bills and their reconciliation against ATCO charges, flagging variances for review.

    Removed a recurring manual calculation cycle and improved billing accuracy and auditability.
  • 1.3

    Automatic tracking of incomplete transactions

    Built automated detection and tracking of incomplete or unacknowledged transactions so exceptions are surfaced early instead of being discovered downstream.

    No transaction falls through the cracks — exceptions are visible the moment they occur.
  • 1.4

    Agile adaptation to webMethods changes

    Rapidly adapted the ingestion and processing pipelines to upstream webMethods changes, keeping transaction flows running without disruption.

    Demonstrated fast, low-risk response to external system change.
  • 2.1

    Retail billing engine built from scratch

    Designed and built the full billing engine: charge calculation, number reconciliation, invoice generation, and cancel & reissue workflows.

    Replaced the legacy process where every customer's PDF invoice had to be produced one by one.
  • 2.2

    Traceable billing history

    Every billing run now records a complete, traceable history automatically — what was billed, when, and under which version.

    Full auditability of every invoice without any manual record-keeping.
  • 2.3

    Automated invoice distribution & accounting records

    Automated invoice distribution and the creation of accounting records, replacing the legacy process where accounts staff emailed invoices and keyed data into MYOB one entry at a time.

    Accounts effort reduced from per-invoice manual work to a single automated flow.
  • 2.4

    Continuous engine improvement

    Continuously evolved the engine to support new contract terms, rebranding and other business changes without disrupting billing cycles.

    The engine grows with the business instead of becoming legacy itself.
  • 2.5

    Requirements elicitation for the retail application

    Led requirements elicitation with business stakeholders for the retail application, translating operational needs into clear, buildable specifications.

    Bridged the gap between business users and the technical build.
  • 2.6

    BAU training & handover

    Designed and delivered training so business users can independently operate the retail application and billing engine to bill customers as business-as-usual.

    The capability now lives with the business, not just with me.
  • 3.1

    Seamless SharePoint → SQL wholesale data capture

    Built a pipeline that captures wholesale data from SharePoint into SQL automatically and continuously.

    Replaced the legacy ADF process that relied on users manually triggering each refresh.
  • 3.2

    MYOB batch export straight after billing

    Accounting data is generated and sent immediately after each billing run, ready to be imported into MYOB as a single batch.

    Accounting data is ready the moment billing completes — no re-keying, no waiting.
  • 3.3

    UAI & UPNA temp-folder housekeeping

    Automated housekeeping of UAI and UPNA temporary folders to prevent file submission rejections from AEMO.

    Eliminated a recurring cause of AEMO rejections before it can occur.
  • 3.4

    UAI & UPNA acknowledgement tracking

    Acknowledgement files received from AEMO are now tracked automatically on arrival, feeding submission-status insight.

    Real-time visibility of market submission status without manual checking.
  • 4.1

    Data tracking dashboard

    Covers APA, AGIG and all other inbound data feeds, with tracking and troubleshooting insights that show exactly where and why data is missing or delayed.

    Data issues are diagnosed in minutes rather than investigated by hand.
  • 4.2

    Portfolio dashboard

    Demand & capacity views, portfolio summary and drill-down detail, with a portfolio simulation capability currently in discussion.

    One place to see the whole portfolio position — and a path toward scenario modelling.
  • 4.3

    Retail billing dashboard

    Consumption report, pricing report, billing preview, bill history, network reconciliation and account reports — the full billing picture in one suite.

    Billing can be previewed, verified and reconciled before anything is issued.
  • 5.1

    Change management plan & process mapping

    Developed the change management plan and mapped current-state processes against the upgraded APA system, identifying every touchpoint that the upgrade affects.

    The business knows exactly what changes, where, and who is impacted — before cut-over.
  • 5.2

    Connectivity testing completed

    Completed connectivity testing against the upgraded APA environment, confirming EMG's integrations are ready for the transition.

    De-risked the upgrade path — integration readiness is verified, not assumed.
